講師使用說明

從真實 DMS 案件出題，有兩條路：DMS 考題用原案件直接考，講師整理畫面、插入題目後就能組卷；DMS 範例由講師設定正解並鎖定判斷依據，再交給 AI 改寫成變化題。專員作答後，選擇題由系統計分，文字作答由 AI 先批改、講師確認再發佈成績。

Trainer Guide

Real DMS cases become quizzes in two ways: a DMS question asks the real case as is (tidy the screen, insert questions, then use it in a quiz); a DMS sample gets its answer and evidence set by a trainer, then the AI rewrites it into variants. Choices are scored automatically; written answers are graded by the AI first and confirmed by trainers.

分頁內容

分頁上的 3 是待審數量。

申請匯入

  1. 勾選 用途，兩個可以都勾：DMS 考題（預設）或 DMS 範例。
  2. Return ID 一行一筆，後面空一格接 判斷時點（台北時間），例如 246771802264043 2026-10-29 13:24。勾選 DMS 考題時一次只能申請一筆。
  3. 判斷時點通常是案件判決前。聊聊、DRC、Operation History、Timeline 只保留這個時間以前的內容，該分鐘內的也會移除；同一分鐘內要保留較早的訊息時，可以填到秒（例如 13:24:20）。其他欄位（例如 Case Status）是案件的最終狀態，可能透露結果，審核時請檢查。沒有填時間的案件要勾 不截斷 才會使用完整資料。
  4. DMS 考題：前幾次申退（選填）填同一筆訂單之前的 Return ID，用逗號分隔。專員作答時可以切換查看。
  5. DMS 範例：選擇 主題，可以勾預期的標籤。
  6. 按 送出申請。排程定時處理（先處理 DMS 考題，再處理範例；同一筆 Return ID 只讀一次 DMS），完成後在 SeaTalk 通知，案件會出現在對應的待審分頁。匯入時不做 AI 判斷、不翻譯。

DMS 有些資料讀不到時（例如物流）案件仍會匯入，相關欄位留空並在提醒中列出；前一次申退讀不到也不會擋住本次。處理中超過 15 分鐘會出現 重試；等待中或失敗的申請可以 取消。

審核 DMS 範例

在「待審 DMS 範例」點範例進入。範例的畫面照 DMS 呈現，正解與判斷依據由講師設定。

範例會顯示什麼（照 DMS）

  • 聊聊：文字、貼圖（顯示貼圖圖片）、買家傳的照片／影片（只留「📷 照片」區塊，不載入圖片）。DMS 不顯示的系統訊息會略過；沒見過的訊息類型顯示成「[系統訊息]」並在提醒中列出代碼，請對照 DMS 後告訴 Chris。
  • DRC：買家第一筆是申請的 System Info（Solution、Refund Amount、Return Reason、Remark）；蝦皮退貨退款小組的訊息標示「蝦皮」。
  • 申請內容：買家申請時附的照片／影片依張數留下區塊；訂單商品、申退商品前面有「商品照」區塊。
  • 照片、影片、貼圖區塊可以填 描述（例如「外箱破損、內容物完好」），影響判斷時再填；描述會跟著送翻譯。

審核進度 5 步

範例頁上方會依序列出要處理的事，做完的步驟打勾：

  1. 正解與判斷重點：在下方題組點選每個小題的正解，在「範例資訊」填判斷重點（也用來區分範例）。
  2. 鎖定判斷依據：在案件內容點 🔓 鎖定決定答案的部分（聊聊、DRC、物流、商品、付款、申退備註、照片都可以鎖）。AI 出變化題時，鎖定的內容只會換句話說，意思和數字不變。申請的方案、金額、原因、折抵與 DRC 提案 AI 一律不會改，不用鎖。
  3. 審核前確認：只列要處理的事（自動遮蔽的內容、格式錯誤、單價 0 的贈品、商品名稱是否含品牌或店家）。純資訊收在「匯入細節」。
  4. 英文翻譯：完成第 1 步後按 送翻譯。
  5. 核准：第 1、3、4 步完成並儲存後才能按。沒有鎖定的範例也能核准，但不能拿來選範例出題。
  • 編輯題目：按 ✎ 編輯題目，案件畫面原地變成可編輯（只改中文）。可以改文字、時間、說話者，插入／刪除／上下移動訊息，新增商品、物流事件、照片；申請金額依申退商品與折抵自動計算。已鎖定的部分唯讀，要先點 🔒 解鎖。按 儲存 存檔並結束編輯，取消 全部還原。
  • 儲存：「儲存」按鈕在有修改時才會亮起；改了又改回去會恢復灰色。離開頁面時不會跳提醒，未儲存的修改會直接放棄。
  • 複製範例：按 ⧉ 複製範例 會複製成新的待審範例（鎖定一起複製），用來自己改出變化版，例如把「有協議」改成「沒協議」，或改數字。列表上會標出來源範例。
  • 退回、刪除：收在 ⋯ 裡。刪除範例時可以同時用新的判斷時點重新申請（重新匯入後，正解、判斷重點、鎖定要重新設定）。
  • DMS 規則：申退原因與申請方案的組合（例如 Damaged item 只能 Return and Refund）會自動檢查。規則在「主題設定 → DMS 規則」維護。

2題庫生成

選幾個範例，請 AI 把每個範例改寫成數題變化題。正解直接沿用範例，講師只要確認內容沒有走樣。

  1. 按 建立草稿：填名稱、選主題，勾選要用的範例並填題數（每個範例預設 5 題）。只能選已核准、而且有鎖定區域的範例；表格會顯示每個範例的判斷重點、正解、鎖定數，以及題庫已有幾題來自它。
  2. 按 複製指令，到 SeaTalk 傳 生成考題 D00x。AI 一批只看一個範例，草稿列表的進度會自動更新。
  3. AI 改寫的規則（系統會自動檢查，不符合就整批退回重寫）：
    • 可以改：買賣家帳號、商品名稱與規格、沒鎖定的訊息（可改寫、合併、刪減）、解析的寫法。
    • 鎖定的訊息：只能換句話說，說話者、時間、先後順序與數字不變。
    • 不能改：申請內容、DRC 提案、蝦皮訊息、照片／貼圖、數量與單價。
    • 系統自動處理：單價與所有金額同比例換算（折抵改成 $30／45／60／90／100 中最接近的），所有日期一起往前或往後挪 7–45 天；正解沿用範例。
  4. 校對：每一題上方有 4 步審核進度（按鈕都在每一步的右上角）：
    • 檢查內容：來自範例鎖定區域的內容會用 🔒 底色標出，請確認意思沒有改變；格式錯誤也列在這裡。
    • 正解：顯示目前的正解（沿用範例 Sxxx）。內容不再支持正解時，修改內容或刪除這題。
    • 英文翻譯：改了中文後按 送翻譯。
    • 確認校對：按 ✓ 確認，自動跳到下一題沒校對的。
  5. 需要修改時按 ✎ 編輯題目 原地修改（和範例頁相同）。「儲存」有修改時才會亮起；換題或離開時不會跳提醒，未儲存的修改直接放棄。刪除這題在 ⋯ 裡。
  6. 批次處理（頁面上方）：全部送翻譯 一次送出所有英文待翻譯的題目；✓ 全部確認 把可以確認的題目一次標記為已校對（略過格式錯誤、正解未設定、英文待翻譯的題目）。建議先抽看幾題再全部確認。
  7. 全部完成後按 加入題庫。英文待翻譯或格式錯誤的題目不能入庫。
  8. 題目都入庫後，管理員可以 刪除草稿，題庫與已發佈的測驗不受影響。校對時刪除單一題目（含「刪除並補題」）所有講師都可以。

批改原則

在「主題設定 → 主題與判斷標準」的 批改原則 設定，AI 批改時一律遵守。預設原則：

  • 點出正確的判斷關鍵就給滿分，不需要引用條文編號或重述案件細節。

5Grading & Release

After the quiz ends, the AI scores written answers first and trainers confirm before releasing.

  1. Open the quiz, copy 批改 E00x and send it on SeaTalk. AI scores appear on the page automatically.
  2. Each written answer gets a score, a reason and a confidence. Answers below the threshold are flagged Low confidence; check those first.
  3. Accept the AI score or enter your own (with an optional comment). Use Accept all unflagged in this question for the rest. The page shows who confirmed each score.
  4. If the AI is too strict or too lenient on a question, adjust the topic's grading principles, press Re-grade unconfirmed scores in this question and send the grading command again. Confirmed scores are kept.
  5. Press Release results. Agents see their score, missed questions and explanations. Unconfirmed answers use the AI score.
Choice answers are scored automatically. You can still change scores after release; agents' results update automatically. Use Withdraw results if needed.

Once results are released you can Archive the quiz on its page: it is hidden from the Quizzes and Grading lists (use "Show archived quizzes" at the bottom to find it); results and history are not affected. Released quizzes moved from V1 are archived automatically.

Grading principles

Set under Settings → Topic & criteria → Grading principles; the AI always follows them. Defaults:

  • Full marks for naming the correct deciding factor; no need to cite clause numbers or repeat case details.
  • Short answers in English or Chinese are fine; no deductions for grammar or spelling.
  • Partial credit when only some of two or more required factors are given; zero or low for a wrong factor, a contradiction with the key, or a conclusion with no reason.

  2. Each question has a tab: 2 is the number not yet graded, 1 the number with low AI confidence, ✓ means all graded.
    • Single choice: scored automatically; see what each agent picked, right or wrong, and how many chose each option.
    • Short answer: one row per agent with the answer, the AI suggestion and the score. For a short answer linked to a choice, an extra column shows what the agent picked.

DMS case quizzes record how long each case was viewed, which blocks and tabs were opened, which photos and videos were opened (and how much was watched), time spent away from the quiz page, and characters pasted into short answers. Agents are told on the quiz screen. An attempt is flagged when:

  • All single choices were right, but 2 or fewer blocks were viewed and no photo or video was opened.
  • The agent came back after 20+ seconds away and answered within 30 seconds.
  • 30+ characters were pasted into a short answer.

A flag is a prompt to look closer, not proof of cheating; read it together with the answers.

Answering

  • Single choice: click one option. Short answer: explain your decision and which part of the case it is based on. A short answer marked "說明第 N 題的選擇" asks why you chose your answer to question N.
  • Answer every question in every case; until you submit you can switch cases and change answers at any time.
  • Answers save automatically (at most every 30 seconds when something changes, and when you switch cases or leave the page). If your computer crashes or the network drops, open the link again to continue; the timer keeps running, so contact your trainer right away.
The quiz records which blocks and tabs you view, which photos and videos you open, time spent away from the quiz page, and pasted text. Your trainer can see this. Read the case and decide on your own; don't discuss it with others.

Submit and results

  • Press 交卷 (Submit); you'll be told which cases still have unanswered questions. The quiz submits automatically when time is up or the trainer ends it.
  • Practice, review any time: answers and explanations appear right after you submit.
  • Practice, review together: answers appear once your trainer shows them.
  • Exam: once your trainer releases results, My quizzes shows your score (out of 100), the answers and explanations.

Help

  • The page will not load or sign-in fails: refresh the page, check you are using your company Google account, then contact your trainer.
  • Something went wrong during the exam (computer crashed, lost connection): reopen the same link on the same computer and press Continue. The timer keeps running, so tell your trainer right away if you need more time.
  • Do not share questions or answers with other agents.
