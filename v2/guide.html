整體流程

後台選單由左到右就是工作順序。

1範例生成DMS 案件 → 範例2題庫生成AI 出題 → 校對3可用題庫已確認的題目4測驗管理抽題 → 發佈5閱卷發佈AI 批改 → 確認6歷史成績成績、補考

登入與權限

  • 用公司 Google 帳號登入平台；講師帳號會進入「講師後台」。右上角可以切換中文 / EN。
  • TW 講師 可以使用全部功能，包含「範例生成」。
  • 所有講師 可以使用「題庫生成」之後的功能。

題型與配分

題型作答方式批改
A　選擇選一個選項系統自動計分
B　選擇 + 原因選一個選項並寫原因選擇自動計分；原因由 AI 評分、講師確認
C　文字作答寫出答案AI 評分、講師確認

每場測驗總分 100 分，系統依題數平均分配。

1範例生成 TW 講師

把真實 DMS 案件變成去識別化的「範例」，AI 出題時會參考這些範例改編。

申請範例（「範例申請」分頁）

  1. 在 Return ID 欄位貼上 DMS 的 Return ID，每行一個，一次最多 30 筆。
  2. 選擇 主題；預期分類（選填） 與 說明（選填） 可以不填，填了可以幫助審核時比對。
  3. 按 送出申請。申請過的 Return ID 會留在列表中；重複送出時系統會自動略過。
  4. 排程每 30 分鐘處理一次：抓 DMS → 去識別化 → AI 翻譯英文並建議分類。完成後 SeaTalk 群組會收到通知。
狀態為「失敗」時可以按 重試；如果案件本身有問題（例如查無資料），取消後換一筆即可。

審核範例（「待審範例」分頁）

  1. 點開範例，確認案件內容（聊聊、DRC、退貨退款申請、訂單商品）沒有殘留店名、帳號等可辨識資訊。
  2. 檢查 AI 建議的 標籤、判斷重點、正解，不對就直接修改。
  3. 確認 變化提示：AI 已預先寫好「哪些地方可以換、哪個判斷關鍵要保留」，講師看過、需要時微調即可。
  4. 按 核准 進入範例庫；不適合當範例就按 退回。
AI 的建議只是起點，正解以講師判斷為準。DMS 案件常有灰色地帶，這正是好範例的來源。

範例庫

  • 左側 主題與標籤 顯示每個標籤有幾筆範例，點一下就能篩選；0 筆的標籤代表這個判斷重點還缺範例。
  • 上方搜尋框可以搜範例代碼、判斷重點、標籤、變化提示。
  • ＋ 標籤 可以新增判斷重點，需要填中英文名稱；說明會給 AI 出題與分類時參考。
  • 滑鼠移到標籤上按 ✎，可以修改名稱、說明，或 停用標籤。已使用這個標籤的範例與題目不受影響；停用後不會再出現在各處的標籤選項，之後可以 重新啟用。

2題庫生成

建立一份「草稿」，請 AI 依範例產生一批題目，講師逐題校對。

  1. 按 建立草稿：填名稱、選主題、題數、題型配比（A / B / C 各幾題），可指定要加強的標籤。
  2. 建立後按 複製指令，到 SeaTalk 對 @RR AI Quiz Assistant 貼上（例如 生成考題 D003）。AI 生成完會在 SeaTalk 回覆。
  3. 回到草稿逐題校對：修改題目、選項、正解、解析、評分標準，按 確認並標記已校對；不要的題目可以刪除。
  4. 校對完成的題目按 加入題庫。
  5. 題目都入庫後可以 刪除草稿，題庫與已發佈的測驗不受影響。
B、C 題的「評分標準」會直接影響 AI 批改的準確度，請寫清楚幾分對應什麼答案。

3可用題庫

所有已確認、可以拿來出測驗的題目。

  • 可以依主題、標籤、題型篩選，並看到每題被用過幾次、答對率。
  • 點開題目可以修改，修改後版本號會增加；已發佈的測驗保留當時的版本，不受影響。
  • 題目過時或有爭議時按 停用，之後抽題就不會再選到。
  • 判斷標準更新後，舊題目會標示 需重新確認，看過並儲存後就會更新為新標準。

4測驗管理

從可用題庫組卷，指定專員與時間後發佈。

  1. 按 發佈新測驗，填測驗名稱、開始 / 結束時間、作答時限（分鐘）。
  2. 貼上受測者 Email，每行一個，可以直接從 Excel 貼上。
  3. 按 自動抽題：依題型配比與標籤挑題；勾選 避開受測者做過的題目 可以避免考到重複題。也可以手動增減題目。
  4. 按 發佈並產生測驗連結，把連結傳給專員。

測驗進行中

  • 可以看到交卷人數；以專員畫面預覽 可以看專員實際看到的畫面（不會留下作答紀錄）。
  • 需要時可以 延長結束時間 或 提前結束測驗。
  • 專員作答時有倒數計時，時間到會自動交卷。

5閱卷發佈

測驗結束後，AI 先批改文字答案，講師確認後發佈成績。

  1. 選擇場次，複製畫面上的指令，到 SeaTalk 對 @RR AI Quiz Assistant 貼上（例如 批改 S005）。
  2. AI 會為每筆文字答案給分、附上理由與信心度。信心度低於門檻的會標示 低信心度，請優先檢查。
  3. 逐筆 接受 AI 分數或改成講師分數（可加評語）；確認沒問題的可以用 本題未標記的全部接受 一次處理。
  4. 全部確認後按 發佈成績，專員就能看到自己的分數與解析。
選擇題由系統自動計分，不需要處理。所有文字答案都有分數後才能發佈。發佈後若發現給錯分，仍可修改，專員的成績會自動更新；必要時也可以按 取消公布。

6歷史成績

查看每場測驗與每位專員的表現。

依測驗

  • 每位專員的分數、交卷時間、是否自動交卷。
  • 點進去可以看逐題作答與評分。
  • 退回：讓專員在測驗開放期間重新作答（原作答保留在紀錄中，不再計分）。
  • 勾選專員後按 為勾選者建立補考：沿用原題目與配分，建立新的場次與連結。

依專員

  • 點專員 Email 查看 歷次成績，掌握進步與弱項。
  • 答對率低於輔導門檻的專員會標示 需輔導，方便安排輔導。

注意事項

  • 資料安全：範例和題目都經過去識別化，帳號、店名、訂單編號、電話、地址等都會被替換或移除；審核時若發現殘留，請直接刪改。Return ID 只保留在申請紀錄，只有 TW 講師看得到，不會出現在題目中。
  • SeaTalk 指令：只需要兩個指令：生成考題 Dxxx 與 批改 Sxxx，都可以在後台畫面直接複製。
  • 速度：後台存取資料需要幾秒鐘，按鈕按下後請稍候，不要重複點擊。專員作答過程不受影響。
  • 問題回報：使用上有任何問題或建議，請聯絡 Chris。

Workflow

The console menu runs left to right in the order you work.

1SamplesDMS case → sample2GenerateAI drafts → review3BankApproved questions4QuizzesPick → publish5GradingAI grades → confirm6HistoryResults, makeups

Sign-in and access

  • Sign in with your company Google account; trainer accounts open the Trainer Console. Use 中文 / EN at the top right to switch the language.
  • TW trainers can use every feature, including Samples.
  • All trainers can use everything from Generate onward.

Question types and points

TypeHow agents answerGrading
A　ChoicePick one optionScored automatically
B　Choice + reasonPick an option and explain whyChoice scored automatically; the reason is scored by AI and confirmed by a trainer
C　WrittenType an answerScored by AI and confirmed by a trainer

Every quiz is worth 100 points, split evenly across its questions.

1Sample Builder TW trainers

Turn real DMS cases into de-identified samples. The AI adapts these samples when it writes questions.

Request samples (Requests tab)

  1. Paste DMS Return IDs into Return IDs, one per line, up to 30 at a time.
  2. Choose the Topic. Expected tags (optional) and Note (optional) help you compare during review.
  3. Press Submit. Requested Return IDs stay in the list, and duplicates are skipped automatically.
  4. A scheduled job runs every 30 minutes: fetch from DMS → de-identify → AI translates to English and suggests tags. The SeaTalk group gets a notice when it finishes.
If a request shows Failed, press Retry. If the case itself is the problem (for example, not found), cancel it and request another one.

Review samples (Pending review tab)

  1. Open a sample and check the case (chat, DRC, return / refund request, order items) for any leftover shop names or usernames.
  2. Check the AI-suggested tags, key point and answer, and edit anything that is wrong.
  3. Check the Variation hint: the AI pre-fills what can be changed and which deciding factor must stay. Adjust it if needed.
  4. Press Approve to add it to the library, or Reject if it is not a good sample.
The AI suggestion is only a starting point; the trainer decides the answer. Grey-area DMS cases make the best samples.

Library

  • Topics & tags on the left shows how many samples each tag has; click one to filter. A tag with 0 samples still needs examples.
  • The search box finds sample IDs, key points, tags and variation hints.
  • ＋ Tag adds a new key point. Enter the Chinese and English names; the description guides the AI when it generates and classifies.
  • Hover over a tag and press ✎ to rename it, change its description, or Disable tag. Samples and questions that use it are not affected; a disabled tag no longer appears in tag options and can be turned back on with Enable again.

2Question Builder

Create a draft, let the AI write a batch of questions from the samples, then review them one by one.

  1. Press New draft: enter a name, choose the topic, the number of questions and the Type mix (how many A / B / C). You can pick tags to focus on.
  2. Press Copy command and send it to @RR AI Quiz Assistant in SeaTalk (for example 生成考題 D003). The assistant replies when it is done.
  3. Review each question: edit the prompt, options, answer, explanation and rubric, then press Confirm & mark reviewed. Delete questions you do not want.
  4. Press Add to bank for the reviewed questions.
  5. Once everything is in the bank you can Delete draft. The bank and published quizzes are not affected.
The rubric for B and C questions directly affects how accurately the AI grades. Spell out which answers earn which points.

3Question Bank

Every approved question that can be used in a quiz.

  • Filter by topic, tag and type, and see how many times each question has been used and its correct rate.
  • Open a question to edit it; its version number goes up. Published quizzes keep the version they used.
  • Press Retire for outdated or disputed questions so they are no longer picked.
  • When the judgment criteria change, older questions are marked Re-check. Review and save them to bring them up to date.

4Quiz Management

Build a quiz from the bank, assign agents and a time window, then publish.

  1. Press Publish a quiz. Enter the title, start / end time and time limit (minutes).
  2. Paste the assignees’ emails, one per line (you can paste straight from Excel).
  3. Press Auto-pick to choose questions by type mix and tags. Tick Avoid questions assignees have taken to prevent repeats. You can also add or remove questions by hand.
  4. Press Publish and create link and send the link to the agents.

While the quiz is running

  • See how many agents have submitted. Preview as agent shows exactly what agents see (nothing is recorded).
  • If needed, use Extend end time or End quiz now.
  • Agents see a countdown, and answers are submitted automatically when time runs out.

5Grading & Release

After the quiz ends, the AI grades written answers first; trainers confirm, then release results.

  1. Choose the quiz, copy the command on screen and send it to @RR AI Quiz Assistant in SeaTalk (for example 批改 S005).
  2. The AI scores each written answer with a reason and a confidence level. Answers below the threshold are flagged Low confidence; check those first.
  3. Accept the AI score or enter your own (with an optional comment). Use Accept all unflagged in this question for the rest.
  4. When everything is confirmed, press Release results. Agents can then see their scores and the explanations.
Choice answers are scored automatically. Results can be released only after every written answer has a score. If you find a mistake after release you can still change the score, and the agent’s result updates automatically; you can also press Withdraw results.

6Results History

See how each quiz and each agent performed.

By quiz

  • Each agent’s score, submit time, and whether it was auto-submitted.
  • Open an attempt to see every answer and its score.
  • Return: let the agent answer again while the quiz is open (the original attempt is kept on record but no longer scored).
  • Tick agents and press Makeup for selected to create a new quiz and link with the same questions and points.

By agent

  • Click an agent’s email to open their History and track progress and weak spots.
  • Agents below the coaching threshold are marked Needs coaching.

Good to know

  • Data protection: samples and questions are de-identified: usernames, shop names, order numbers, phone numbers and addresses are replaced or removed. If you spot anything left over during review, edit it out. Return IDs stay only in the request list, visible to TW trainers, and never appear in questions.
  • SeaTalk commands: there are only two, 生成考題 Dxxx and 批改 Sxxx, and both can be copied from the console.
  • Speed: the console takes a few seconds to load data. Wait after pressing a button instead of clicking again. Agents’ answering is not affected.
  • Questions or feedback: contact Chris.

流程總覽

從收到連結到查看成績，共六個步驟。

1登入公司 Google 帳號2開始作答開始計時3閱讀案件看清楚案件4作答選擇 / 文字5交卷確定交卷6查看成績分數與解析
請用電腦作答，不要用手機；使用一般（非無痕）瀏覽器視窗，開始前確認網路穩定。

1登入

  1. 打開講師提供的測驗連結。
  2. 用公司 Google 帳號（@shopeemobile-external.com）登入。
  3. 進入 我的測驗，會列出指派給你的所有測驗，以及測驗期間、作答時限和題數。
  4. 右上角的 中文 / EN 可以切換語言。
如果畫面顯示 目前沒有指派給你的測驗，請確認登入的帳號是否正確，再聯絡講師。

2開始作答

  • 只有在 測驗期間 內才能開始；還沒開放時，畫面會顯示開放時間。
  • 閱讀 開始前請注意，再按 開始作答。
  • 計時會立即開始，無法暫停；剩餘時間會一直顯示在畫面上方。

3閱讀案件

每一題都是一筆退貨退款案件。

區塊內容
買家與賣家帳號與 ID（虛構，不是真實人物）
訂單商品訂購的商品，以及付款和物流時間軸
聊聊買家與賣家之間的對話
DRC各方與蝦皮之間的溝通，不是買賣雙方的對話。
退貨退款申請方案、金額、原因、備註與申退商品。折抵（折價券 / 蝦幣）是從金額中扣掉的折扣。
請把聊聊從頭看到最後再判斷，並依受訓時學到的判斷標準作答。

4作答

題型作答方式
選擇選一個選項。
選擇 + 原因選一個選項，再寫下你的判斷原因；原因也會計分。
文字作答寫出你的答案。
  • 每題旁邊會顯示配分，整份測驗共 100 分。
  • 寫原因或文字答案時，請說明是案件中的哪個內容讓你這樣判斷（例如哪一則聊聊）。可以用英文作答，文法與拼字不扣分。
  • 交卷前可以回到任何一題修改答案；畫面上方會顯示 已作答 題數。
  • 答案會暫存在這個瀏覽器。作答期間請不要關閉頁面、換電腦或清除瀏覽器資料。

5交卷

  1. 按 交卷，確認畫面會顯示還有幾題沒作答。
  2. 按 確定交卷。交卷後就不能再修改答案。
  • 剩下 5 分鐘 和 1 分鐘 時會跳出提醒。
  • 時間到，或講師提前結束測驗時，系統會自動交卷。
  • 如果因為網路問題交卷失敗，頁面會每 15 秒自動重試；請保持頁面開啟，直到看到 已交卷。

6查看成績

  • 講師會先確認文字答案的分數，所以交卷後不會馬上看到成績。
  • 成績公布後，到 我的測驗 按 查看，就能看到分數、答錯的題目與解析。
  • 講師可能會請你重新作答或參加補考，新的測驗會出現在「我的測驗」。

遇到問題

  • 頁面打不開或無法登入：重新整理頁面，確認使用的是公司 Google 帳號，仍無法解決請聯絡講師。
  • 作答中途出狀況（電腦當機、網路斷線）：用同一台電腦重新打開同一個連結，按 繼續作答。計時不會停止，需要更多時間請立刻聯絡講師。
  • 請勿將題目或答案分享給其他專員。

Overview

Six steps from the link to your results.

1Sign inCompany Google2StartTimer begins3ReadCheck the case4AnswerChoice / written5SubmitFinish exam6ResultsScore & review
Use a computer, not a phone, and a normal (not incognito) browser window. Make sure your internet connection is stable before you start.

1Sign in

  1. Open the quiz link from your trainer.
  2. Sign in with your company Google account (@shopeemobile-external.com).
  3. You will see My quizzes: every quiz assigned to you, with its exam window, time limit and number of questions.
  4. Use 中文 / EN at the top right to switch the language.
If you see No quiz assigned to you yet, check that you signed in with the right account, then contact your trainer.

2Start the exam

  • You can only start during the Exam window. Before it opens, the page shows when it opens.
  • Read Before you start, then press Start exam.
  • The timer starts immediately and cannot be paused. The time left is always shown at the top.

3Read the case

Each question is based on a return / refund case.

SectionWhat it shows
Buyer & sellerUsernames and IDs (made up, not real people)
Order itemsWhat was ordered, plus payment and the logistics timeline
ChatThe conversation between the buyer and the seller
DRCEach party’s channel with Shopee. It is not a conversation between buyer and seller.
Return / refund requestSolution, amount, reason, remark and requested items. Deduction (voucher / coins) is the discount taken off the amount.
Read the whole chat to the end before you decide, and judge by the criteria from your training.

4Answer

TypeHow to answer
ChoicePick one option.
Choice + reasonPick one option, then explain how you decided. The reason is scored too.
WrittenType your answer.
  • The points for each question are shown next to it. The whole exam is worth 100 points.
  • For reasons and written answers, say what in the case made you decide (for example, which chat message). English is fine; grammar and spelling are not scored.
  • You can go back to any question and change your answer before you finish. The top bar shows Answered so far.
  • Answers are saved in this browser. Do not close the page, switch computers or clear your browser while answering.

5Submit

  1. Press Finish exam. The confirmation shows how many questions are not answered yet.
  2. Press Submit. You cannot change your answers after submitting.
  • You get a warning when 5 minutes and 1 minute are left.
  • When time runs out, or if your trainer ends the quiz early, your answers are submitted automatically.
  • If submitting fails because of the network, the page retries every 15 seconds. Keep the page open until you see Submitted.

6Results

  • Your trainer reviews written answers first, so results are not shown right away.
  • When results are released, open My quizzes and press View to see your score, the questions you missed and the explanations.
  • Your trainer may ask you to answer again or take a makeup quiz. It will appear in My quizzes.
