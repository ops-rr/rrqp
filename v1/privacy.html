RR AI Quiz Platform — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

RR AI Quiz Platform is an internal training quiz tool for the Returns & Refunds team and its partner agents.

Information we collect

  • Your Google account email address and name, obtained through Google Sign-In, used only to identify you and determine your role (trainer or agent).
  • Your quiz answers, scores, and submission times.

How we use it

  • To deliver assigned quizzes, grade answers, and show results to you and your trainers for coaching.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the platform administrator. Quiz content is fictional training material. Records are kept only as long as needed for training purposes and can be deleted on request.

Google user data

The app requests only basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). It does not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: faster4869@gmail.com
